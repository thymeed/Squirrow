Squirrow

A local-first memory manager for your AI chats. Save what your AI should remember, then paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or any assistant with one click. No account, and nothing leaves your browser.

How it works

  1. Create a project, one per thing you are building.
  2. Add entries: facts, decisions, preferences, snippets and file notes.
  3. Tick the ones you want, check the token estimate, and copy a ready-to-paste Markdown block.

Questions

Where is my data stored?

In your browser's IndexedDB on your own device. There is no backend, analytics or account.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Install it from your browser and it opens without a network.

Can I back up or move my data?

Yes. Export everything as JSON or Markdown, and import the JSON on another device.
